Privacy Policy

1. Introduction and Scope

At Shikela, accessible via our web and mobile platforms, the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary concerns. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal information that is collected, recorded, and utilized by Shikela. Because we provide daily online job vacancies, profitable business tips, and tech advice targeting users in Africa, Ethiopia, and globally, we are committed to ensuring your digital footprint is handled securely and transparently.

If you have additional questions, require more information about our privacy practices, or wish to exercise your data rights, please do not hesitate to contact our support team through our official Contact Us page.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with tailored tech updates, relevant job postings, and a seamless browsing experience, we collect specific information when you interact with our platform. This data falls into two main categories:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you contact us directly via email or our support forms, we may receive personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of the message, and any attachments you send us.
  • Automatically Collected Information: When you navigate Shikela to read our daily business tips or social media guides, our servers automatically collect standard non-identifying data. This includes your browser type, device type (mobile or desktop), operating system, language preference, referring/exit pages, and the date and time of your visits.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Shikela platform and its daily content updates.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website to better serve job seekers in Ethiopia and across Africa.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our platform to develop new products, services, and features.
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through one of our partners, to provide customer service and support.
  • Find and prevent fraud, ensuring the integrity of our Secure Matrix.
  • Serve relevant advertisements and track the performance of ad campaigns (see AdSense section below).

4. Log Files

Shikela follows a standard and secure procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites—a standard practice for hosting companies and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These data points are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is strictly for analyzing user trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement around the website, and gathering broad demographic information.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any modern, professional website, Shikela uses 'cookies'. These cookies are small text files used to store information, including visitors' preferences and the specific pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content—such as our daily job and tech posts—based on visitors' browser types, device dimensions, and other localized information.

6. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Google AdSense

To keep our daily business tips and job posting services entirely free for our users, Shikela monetizes the platform through third-party advertising, primarily Google AdSense. Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, specifically known as DART cookies, to serve targeted ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to Shikela and other websites on the internet.

However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Shikela, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit. Please note that Shikela has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

7. External Links and Third-Party Job Portals

Shikela acts as an aggregator, meaning we post daily online work vacancies from across the globe. Consequently, our site contains numerous external links to third-party recruitment sites, company career pages, and external digital resources. Shikela's Privacy Policy does not apply to these external websites. We strongly advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party platforms before submitting your resume, personal information, or application data to them.

8. User Data Rights (CCPA & GDPR)

We believe in full transparency regarding your data. Depending on your region, you may be entitled to the following data protection rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data that we have collected.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

9. Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

Another core part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Shikela does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

This Privacy Policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in Shikela. This policy is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website. Last Updated: August 2026.
